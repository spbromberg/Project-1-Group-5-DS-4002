Conclusion

The results of this analysis show that positive and negative IMDb reviews contain distinguishable language patterns. Review length alone does not appear to be a useful indicator of sentiment because positive and negative reviews have very similar word counts.

TF-IDF performed better than Word Count during cross-validation and was used for the final logistic regression model. The final model successfully classified positive and negative reviews and exceeded the project's target of at least 85% test accuracy.

The most influential words also showed clear differences between the two sentiment groups. Positive reviews were associated with words such as "great," "excellent," "perfect," and "wonderful," while negative reviews were associated with words such as "worst," "waste," "awful," and "boring."

Overall, the results support the conclusion that specific word usage provides more useful information for distinguishing positive and negative IMDb reviews than review length alone.
